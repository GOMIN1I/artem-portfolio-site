Online shopping for people over sixty.

I took part in a hackathon and came up with this idea there: a marketplace that older people can use on their own, without calling anyone for help.

On the desk: a hackathon idea

Ryadom: shopping that keeps family close.

The name means “nearby”. The concept answers the three reasons older people give up on marketplaces: small buttons, fear of a mistake, and nobody around to help.

  1. Voice first. Search starts with a microphone, categories are pictures, prices are the biggest text on screen.
  2. Practice without risk. A training purchase walks through the whole order with no real payment or delivery.
  3. Family one tap away. Share a basket with a relative, open theirs, and learn to spot a dangerous request.

Concept screens from the hackathon. Screen text is in Russian, the language of the people it was made for.
